Our Heritage

Built on Experience.
Driven by Commitment.

Founded in 1985 by Mr. Chanderkumar Kapoor, Suraj Logistics is a family-owned transportation company rooted in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

In 2008, Karan Kapoor joined the family business as the second generation, carrying forward its commitment to dependable transport solutions and lasting customer relationships.

From industrial cargo to complex heavy and over-dimensional consignments, we combine practical expertise with a fleet of 35 owned vehicles.
