Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 27 November 2026

Suraj Logistics respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with our website and transportation services.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us through telephone, email or WhatsApp, we may receive information such as:

  • Name and company name
  • Mobile number and email address
  • Loading and unloading addresses
  • Consignment and transportation details
  • Business enquiries and related documents

2. How We Use Your Information

Customer information is used for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to transportation enquiries
  • Preparing freight quotations
  • Vehicle placement and shipment coordination
  • Delivery updates and customer support
  • Billing and business documentation
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

Suraj Logistics does not sell customer personal information to third parties for advertising purposes.

Information may be shared where necessary with drivers, transport partners, service providers or government authorities for transportation operations or legal compliance.

4. Data Security

We are committed to taking reasonable precautions to protect customer information against unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure or loss. However, no electronic communication or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Cookies and Website Hosting

Our website does not currently use Google Analytics or an online enquiry form.

Our website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses and browser details, for security and website operation.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may provide links to WhatsApp and other external services. These services are governed by their respective privacy policies. We encourage visitors to review those policies before sharing information.

7. Data Retention

Customer information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for business operations, accounting, contractual requirements and applicable legal obligations.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, customers may request access to, correction or deletion of their personal information, withdraw consent where applicable, or raise a privacy related grievance by contacting us.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Suraj Logistics may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in business practices or legal requirements. The revised policy will be published on this website.
